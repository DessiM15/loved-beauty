Good to know

  • The countdown runs to 31 October 2026. That date is a placeholder until the launch date is set.
  • “Dramatic reveal” plays at the end of the film. Press “Play the ending” to skip to it.
  • The email box shows its thank-you message here but does not save addresses yet.
  • The film starts silent. The round buttons on it pause it and turn the sound on.
  • On a phone the film fills the screen in all four layouts, so only Mood and “Coming Soon” change the phone view.
  • Full screen crops the film on wide screens. Split and Portrait show the whole picture. Three panel fills a wide screen with three moments of the film side by side, trimming no more than a sliver at the edges.
  • Her pink logo is faint on the cream panel in Split · Light, as it is in the store’s menu bar.
